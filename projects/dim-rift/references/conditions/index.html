Blessed: A card is "Blessed" while a creature with at least 1 mana connects to it. The "Blessed" effect is active while the card is "Blessed".

Conduit: A card connected to [this] is connected to everything [this] connects to.

Inspired: The card can perform an extra Action each Event, but not the same Action twice.

Prone: Any Action performed by a Prone creature has its effect replaced with: "Get Up - [this] clears Prone."

Protected: The creature cannot receive damage.

Suppressed: Ignore diagonal arrows and consider orthogonal arrows at most single arrows.

Weak: The creature has -2 Might (min 0). It does not stack.
