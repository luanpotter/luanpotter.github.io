Movement

Reorient

All creatures always have the Reorient action in their Action Pool, regardless of event choice. Reorient allows a creature to rotate in place to a different orientation without moving to a different zone.

March

All your face-up creatures get the March action added to your pool when you choose the Campaign Event.

Target a different empty, conquered creature zone you control: move this card to it, selecting any desired orientation. The destination zone must be targeted beforehand, but the final rotation can be chosen at resolution. A card can "jump over" other cards like a chess Knight; but it cannot move to an occupied zone.

March must target a zone other than the one the creature is currently in. To simply rotate in place, use Reorient instead.

Creatures with Explorer can target any (conquered or non-conquered) zones you control to move, regardless of their arrows.
