Other Actions

Creatures might have myriad of other actions on their Action Pool, some bespoke, some common. Here are some common ones:

  • Equip: Attach an Equipment to target creature
  • Conscript: Summon a Creature Token to target empty conquered creature zone you control
  • Pray: Collect 1 mana on this card
  • Burst: Deal 1 damage to all connected face-up creatures
