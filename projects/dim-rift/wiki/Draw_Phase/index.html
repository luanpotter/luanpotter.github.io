Draw Phase

The Draw Phase is the last part of a turn, following the Deployment Phase, in accordance to the Turn Structure.

This is the "end phase" of the turn. It serves two purposes:

  • the turn player must Draw one card from their Main Deck into their hand. This is mandatory if there are any cards left to draw, but decking out does not cause a game loss. Drawing at the end of your turn is the main one of the three ways to draw cards;
  • effects that trigger "at the end of the turn", and other Fast Speed effects, are resolved here.

After the Draw Phase, the turn ends and the next player's turn begins, starting with their Begin Phase.
