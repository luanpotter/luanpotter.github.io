Hand

The Hand is one of the places in which cards can be; each player has one. It has no relevant order and is secret to the opponent.

Cards enter the hand only by being drawn from the Main Deck, starting with each player's starting hand (see Turn Structure and Mulligan).

Cards leave the hand by being summoned or set during the Deployment Phase, or by being discarded.
