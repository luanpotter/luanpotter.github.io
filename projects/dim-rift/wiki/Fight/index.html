Fight

A fight happens when a Strike is blocked: the attacking creature and its blocker fight each other.

In a fight, both creatures deal damage equal to their Might Stat to each other simultaneously. If at any point (as a state-based action) the damage counters on a creature equal or exceed their Life Stat, they are destroyed.

Damage to creatures NEVER "pierces" (i.e. extra damage does not bleed to the player).

First Strike

During a Battle Event, the attacking creature has First Strike against active blockers. This means the attacker deals its damage first; if the active blocker is destroyed before it can deal damage back, the attacker takes no damage. Passive blocks (where the creature in the targeted zone must block) remain simultaneous as normal.
